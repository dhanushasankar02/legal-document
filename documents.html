Valid State Statute

LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT OF JONATHAN EDWARD VANCE

I, Jonathan Edward Vance, residing in the State of California, declare that this instrument is my Last Will and Testament, revoking all prior wills and codicils made by me.

ARTICLE I: EXECUTOR APPOINTMENT
I hereby nominate and appoint my trusted representative as Executor of this Will. The Executor shall serve without bond requirement to the extent permitted under California Probate Code § 8481.

ARTICLE II: GUARDIANSHIP PROVISIONS
In the event of my death leaving minor children, I nominate my designated legal guardian to exercise custody and care of such minors.

Executed under legal seal on this 7th day of September, 2026.
