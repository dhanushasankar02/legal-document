Process Answers

Frequently Asked Process Questions

Is a document created online legally binding? +
Yes! An online Will or Trust is 100% legally binding provided it is executed according to your state's signing, witness, and notarization statutes.
Can I edit my document after downloading it? +
Absolutely. You can log into your JurisDraft dashboard anytime to make changes, update beneficiaries, or re-download your updated legal documents for free.
What is the difference between a Will and a Living Trust? +
A Last Will takes effect after death and passes through probate court. A Revocable Living Trust takes effect immediately, manages assets privately, and bypasses probate court entirely.
